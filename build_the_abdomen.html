Build the Digestive System

Rebuild the abdominal model by dragging each organ into its correct position. Work at your own level: Guide me, I’m confident, or Test me.

How to play

Mouse or touch: drag a piece into the body outline.
Click method: select a piece, then (in Guide me) click its matching outline.
Keyboard: Tab to a piece, arrow keys to move (hold Shift for bigger steps), Enter to drop.
Layers: deep organs (kidneys, pancreas) sit behind others — place them before the organs that cover them. Click a placed organ (or press Delete) to take it back out.
